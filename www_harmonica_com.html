Moon River

Louis Armstrong

Song facts: “Moon River” won Audrey Hepbrun an Academy Award in 1961 for her rendition of the song in Breakfast At Tiffany’s . When an executive involved with Breakfast At Tiffany’s suggested deleting the song from the film, Audrey Hepburn reacted with words to the effect of  “over my dead body”.

Harmonica type: diatonic
